# Sources

## Conselho Nacional de Justiça (CNJ)

**Resolução nº 175, de 14 de maio de 2013.** Official legal source for the nationwide standardization of civil marriage procedures for same-sex couples.

- Resolution: https://atos.cnj.jus.br/atos/detalhar/atos-normativos?documento=1754
- CNJ explanatory note: https://www.cnj.jus.br/resolucao-que-disciplina-a-atuacao-dos-cartorios-no-casamento-gay-entra-em-vigor-amanha/

## Fundação Seade

**Panorama dos casamentos civis de pessoas do mesmo sexo.** Seade Informa — Demografia, June 2025.

Used for São Paulo state figures, including 37,625 accumulated same-sex civil marriages from 2013 to 2024, the increase in their share of all marriages from 0.7% to 1.8%, and the 65% female-couple share in 2024.

- https://informa.seade.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/seade-informa-demografia-panorama-casamentos-mesmo-sexo.pdf

## IBGE

**Estatísticas do Registro Civil 2024.** Used for national totals and the sex composition of same-sex marriages.

The project uses: 948,925 civil marriages in total in 2024; 12,187 same-sex marriages in 2024; 11,198 in 2023; 8.8% growth from 2023 to 2024; and 64.6% female couples among same-sex marriages in 2024.

- Main publication page: https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/sociais/populacao/9110-estatisticas-do-registro-civil.html
- IBGE Educa summary: https://educa.ibge.gov.br/criancas/voce-sabia/23165-numero-de-casamentos.html

## CRC Nacional / Arpen-Brasil — local figures reproduced in reporting

The local Piracicaba figures were published as CRC Nacional / Arpen-Brasil data in local reporting. The repository does not claim these two articles are primary registry extracts.

### Sampi — 28 May 2023

Reports the local annual sequence: 2013=17, 2014=22, 2015=18, 2016=16, 2017=22, 2018=22, 2019=37, 2020=20, 2021=19, 2022=34, and 12 registrations through April 2023. It also reports 239 accumulated marriages through April 2023, of which 153 were between two women.

- https://sampi.net.br/piracicaba/noticias/2764128/cidade/2023/05/numero-de-casamentos-homoafetivos-cresce-em-piracicaba-mulheres-lideram

### Sampi — 3 July 2025

Reports 62 same-sex marriages in Piracicaba in 2024, described as the highest local value, and uses 17 as the 2014 baseline when describing growth above 250% over ten years. This baseline conflicts with the detailed 2023 sequence, which reports 2014=22.

- https://sampi.net.br/piracicaba/noticias/2913445/cidade/2025/07/casamentos-lgbtqia-crescem-mais-de-250-em-10-anos-em-piracicaba
